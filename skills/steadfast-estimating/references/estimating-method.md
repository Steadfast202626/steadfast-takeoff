# Estimating method

## Intake

Confirm these, or list what is missing. Never assume.

- Address, year built, occupied or vacant, access and parking.
- Scope by room or area, with measurements or drawings.
- Customer selections made and not made. Finish level: builder, mid or high.
- Structural, plumbing, electrical, HVAC or egress changes.
- Permits needed: building, plumbing, gas, electrical, and a development permit for a legal suite.
- Existing conditions: moisture, cracks, old wiring, and possible asbestos or vermiculite in buildings from before 1990 (flag for testing before demolition).
- The customer's timing driver and budget range.
- Commercial: drawing and specification revision, tender documents, contract form, schedule, landlord rules, after-hours work, occupied premises.

## Estimate class

State the class on every estimate.

| Class | Accuracy | Basis | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | ±25 to 30% | Phone call or photos | Never sent as a price |
| C | ±15 to 20% | Site visit, no drawings | Budget number only |
| B | ±10% | Site visit with measurements and most selections | Can be quoted |
| A | ±5% | Full scope, drawings, selections, current trade quotes | Can be quoted |

## Building the estimate

Build it line by line the way the contract will read, so the four stage invoices carve cleanly from it.

1. **Scope.** Room by room (residential) or by CSI MasterFormat division (commercial).
2. **Takeoff.** Quantity, unit and waste factor on every line, with the maths shown.
3. **Pricing.** From the price book and current trade quotes only. A missing cost becomes a marked assumption or stays blank, and goes on Kamal's list.
4. **Labour.** Crew, hours and rate per line. Adjust productivity for occupied homes, tight access, older buildings and winter work.
5. **Trades.** Level every quote side by side. Call out scope gaps, exclusions and missing WCB clearance or insurance.
6. **General requirements.** Permits and inspections, disposal bins, site protection, temporary heat or power, clean-up, supervision time, travel markup outside Edmonton and area.
7. **Allowances** for anything not yet selected, each with an amount and what it covers.
8. **Contingency.** The owners' rate for residential or commercial, raised for older homes, hidden conditions or incomplete drawings. Explain why.
9. **Overhead and margin.** Residential floor 30%, target 35%. Commercial floor set by the owners. Show cost, markup and margin. Below the floor goes to the owners.
10. **Exclusions, assumptions and clarifications**, written for the customer.
11. **GST** shown separately.

## Checks against reality

- Cost per square foot against past Steadfast jobs and post-mortems. Say plainly when there is no real job cost data to compare against.
- Trade share of cost (for example drywall, flooring, electrical as a share of the total) against past jobs.
- Change order history on similar jobs.

## Risk review

On every estimate, list the top five risks to cost or schedule. For each: the likely cost if it happens and how much of it is already in the price.

## Outputs

- **Internal estimate:** full costs, labour, markup, margin, class, assumptions and risk review. Owners only.
- **Customer quote:** scope, price, allowances, exclusions, clarifications and the payment schedule. No costs or margin.
- **Bid-levelling sheet** for trade quotes.
- **Scope letters** for trades, listing exactly what each quote must include, with quantities from the takeoff, and asking for a WCB clearance letter and a liability insurance certificate.
- **RFIs** for anything unclear on the drawings.

## Residential payment schedule

25% at signed contract, 35% when the supplier confirms the materials order, 30% when installation starts on site, 10% after the customer signs the deficiency list. Each stage plus GST.

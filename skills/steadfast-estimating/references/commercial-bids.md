# Commercial bids

Every commercial number stays a draft until an owner signs off. Steadfast does not quote commercial work routinely until post-mortems show residential estimates landing within 5% of actual cost, unless both owners decide otherwise in writing for a specific bid.

## Tender package checklist

Ask for anything missing:

- Full drawing set and specifications, with the revision of each.
- Addenda, numbered. Confirm every addendum is in the price.
- Instructions to bidders, the bid form, and the bid due date, time and method of submission.
- Question (RFI) deadline and mandatory site meeting date.
- Contract form (CCDC 2 or other) and supplementary conditions.
- Schedule: start, completion, phasing, working hours, occupied areas, liquidated damages.
- Insurance limits, WCB clearance, bonding, and site safety requirements.
- Allowances, alternates and unit prices requested on the bid form.
- Payment terms and holdback.

## Flag on every commercial estimate

- **Contract form** and the risk each clause places on Steadfast: indemnities, liquidated damages, warranty length, termination, set-off.
- **Holdback and payment.** Alberta's Prompt Payment and Construction Lien Act: proper invoices at least every 31 days, owner payment within 28 days of a proper invoice, subcontractors paid within 7 days of Steadfast being paid, 10% holdback, lien period generally 60 days. Show what this does to Steadfast's bank balance using the cash flow rules in the format file. Check the actual contract, which may differ.
- **Bonding and insurance** limits, and whether Steadfast can meet them.
- **WCB.** Whether commercial work changes Steadfast's WCB classification or rate. Confirm with WCB Alberta.
- **Capacity.** Whether Steadfast can staff and carry the job without hurting residential work.
- **Base bid and alternates.** Where the client wants necessary work kept apart from cosmetic work, price the necessary work as the base bid and the cosmetic work as alternates. Never cut margin to hit a number.

## Sources to check (current versions)

- Alberta Prompt Payment and Construction Lien Act and regulation.
- WCB Alberta, coverage for contractors and subcontractors.
- National Building Code, 2023 Alberta Edition.
- City of Edmonton and Leduc permit fee schedules.

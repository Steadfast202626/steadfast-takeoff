Who this is for

Steadfast takeoff is an internal tool of Steadfast Constructions Ltd. Only Steadfast's owners, staff and the people they invite may use it. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

The app measures drawings and prepares estimates and quotes. It saves jobs to Steadfast's Monday.com account. When Steadfast connects QuickBooks Online, the app reads bills, vendors, items and project costs to check prices and compare estimates with actual costs.

QuickBooks Online

  • The app only reads from QuickBooks. It never creates, changes or deletes anything there.
  • Only someone signed in to the app can connect or disconnect QuickBooks.
  • You can disconnect at any time, from the app or from the Apps page in QuickBooks.

Your responsibility

Prices, quantities and quotes from the app are working figures. A Steadfast owner checks and approves every price before it goes to a customer.

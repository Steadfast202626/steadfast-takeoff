Privacy

Steadfast takeoff. Last updated 2026-10-23.

What the app holds

  • Job details: client name, contact, site address, drawings, measurements, estimates and quotes.
  • Steadfast's price book and building rules.
  • From QuickBooks Online, when connected: bills, vendors, items and project costs. The app reads these. It never changes them.

Why

To prepare estimates and quotes, keep prices current, and compare what a job was estimated to cost with what it cost.

Where it is kept

  • Jobs, the price book and reviews are saved in Steadfast's Monday.com account.
  • The QuickBooks sign-in tokens are kept on the app's server (Netlify), encrypted. They are never sent to a web browser.
  • Your browser keeps a working copy of the open job and its drawings so the app runs quickly.

Who can see it

Only Steadfast's owners and staff who sign in to the app. Steadfast does not sell this information or share it for advertising. Costs, margins and the price book are never shown in anything sent to a customer.

Disconnecting QuickBooks

Disconnect from the app or from the Apps page in QuickBooks. Either way the app can no longer read anything. Disconnecting from the app deletes its saved QuickBooks tokens at once; disconnecting from QuickBooks makes them useless, and the app deletes them the next time it tries to use them.
